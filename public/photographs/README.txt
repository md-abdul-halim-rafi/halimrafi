Photographs by Halim Rafi.

Filenames describe what each file contains. The names first suggested had the
marina and the riprap the other way round, so the files were renamed after being
opened and checked:

  marina-breakwater.jpg    masts, the breakwater, port lights across the water
  riprap-still-water.jpg   riprap boulders meeting still water
  pier-shed-railings.jpg   pier shed and white railings in warm light
  half-moon-sky.jpg        a half moon, most of the frame given to sky

Originals are untouched: full resolution, EXIF intact. The site reads their real
dimensions and rotation from the files, so the reserved box always matches.

  sea-lavender.jpg         sea lavender in focus, boardwalk and walkers behind

All five are present. Nothing is missing.

Note: never write test or placeholder content to paths under this directory.
Doing so leaves the image optimizer and the browser holding the wrong bytes for
a real photograph's URL, which is why several files carry renamed paths.
